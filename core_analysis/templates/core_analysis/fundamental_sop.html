Morningstar Tab SOP

Short methodology note for the Fundamental Analysis desk. The tab uses reported NEPSE fundamentals to build valuation, quality, peer-rank, and growth/value views. It is a heuristic research screen, not an official Morningstar rating and not investment advice.

1. Morningstar tab

The tab answers three quick research questions:

  • Is the stock cheap or expensive versus its own fundamentals?
  • How does it rank against same-sector peers?
  • Which sector names look stronger on the Growth & Value model?

The output should be used as a screening layer before reading the full financial statement rows.

3. Formula quick guide

MetricCalculationInterpretation
Price / Bookprice / BVPSLower is cheaper, assuming book value quality is comparable.
Dividend YieldDPS / priceHigher is better when dividend is sustainable.

Growth & Value ScoreWeighted growth and value sub-scores, scaled 0-100Strong when both growth and value are above the configured threshold.

4. Operating steps

  • Open Fundamental Analysis and find a company by symbol, company name, or sector. Results are grouped by sector; direct symbol entry remains available.
  • Check the financial statement tab first for period, sector, and source context.
  • Open Morningstar using the tab. Review fair value, peer ranks, and the Growth & Value table.
  • Use filters for score, segment, and ticker when comparing the sector model.
  • Treat the result as a shortlist, then verify with the raw statement rows and market context.

5. Limits

The model is only as fresh as the synced financial statements and EOD data. Thinly traded names, stale prices, missing statement rows, sector-specific accounting differences, and one-off earnings can distort scores.

This is a descriptive research aid. It does not forecast price, assign an official rating, or replace independent valuation work.
