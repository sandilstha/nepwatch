Fundamentals Sync

Pick a sector to pull its companies' latest published quarters from funda.aurasrp.com.np. Tick specific companies to sync only those — leave them all unticked and the whole sector runs. Each sync stores a snapshot and pushes the statements into the Financial Statement, so the fundamentals desk, Morningstar and Stock 360 update too.

{% comment %} The dropdown is built from the sectors that actually HAVE statement rows, so Mutual Fund is absent by construction rather than by omission. Saying so here stops it reading as a missing sector or a broken sync — funds file no statements at all, and their equivalent is the NAV feed. {% endcomment %}

Mutual funds are not in this list, and should not be. A fund files no balance sheet or income statement — it has no revenue or ROE — so there is nothing here to pull for one. Their equivalent is NAV and discount to NAV: use Sync Fund NAV in the toolbar above. Debentures and preference shares are absent for the same reason.
